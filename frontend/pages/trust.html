TRUST & SAFETY

Trust is part of the Core.

BISOVIA is designed with shared trust and safety capabilities so users and service providers can participate with clear community standards and responsible interactions.

⭐

Reputation

Service interactions can contribute to a user's reputation within the BISOVIA ecosystem.

💬

Reviews

Users can provide feedback about eligible interactions and services as the platform develops.

🛡️

Safety

Shared safety mechanisms provide a foundation for responsible participation across BISOVIA service modules.

🚩

Reports

Users will be able to report inappropriate or problematic interactions through the platform's reporting system.

Community principles

Trust & Safety is designed around practical principles that can be shared across Mobility, Auto & Garages, Hospitality and Community.

1
Respect Interactions should remain respectful and appropriate for the people involved.
2
Transparency Service information, interactions and relevant feedback should be presented clearly.
3
Accountability Users and service providers should remain responsible for their participation on the platform.
4
Responsible participation Community interaction should support useful, safe and constructive participation.

Report an issue

A reporting workflow will allow users to notify BISOVIA about problematic interactions, service concerns or other issues requiring review.

One Trust & Safety foundation.

Trust, reputation, reviews, reports and moderation belong to the BISOVIA Core so that service modules can use a consistent trust system instead of creating separate systems.
