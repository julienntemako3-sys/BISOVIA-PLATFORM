BISOVIA SERVICES

Useful services, connected in one platform.

BISOVIA is being developed to bring practical service areas together in one clear environment, with each major service introduced progressively as its foundation becomes ready.

SERVICE AREAS

Designed around real-world needs.

These service areas describe the direction of BISOVIA. They will be activated progressively and should not be understood as fully operational services until their respective modules are completed and tested.

◆

Transport & Mobility

A planned utility area for practical mobility and transport services, with future support for Pi-based service experiences.

⚙

Vehicle & Garage Services

A planned area connecting vehicle users with useful maintenance, assistance and garage-related services.

⌂

Hospitality

A future service area for accommodation, meals and related hospitality needs, introduced when the required infrastructure is ready.

◈

Digital Utilities

Practical digital tools and additional utility modules can be introduced progressively as BISOVIA expands.

SERVICE DEVELOPMENT

Services will be activated progressively.

BISOVIA will clearly distinguish planned services from active functionality. Each major module will be developed, tested and connected to the appropriate Pi infrastructure before being presented as an operational service.
