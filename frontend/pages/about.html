ABOUT BISOVIA

Built for practical utility.

BISOVIA is a Pi-native utility platform built to connect people with practical digital services and community interaction through one expanding ecosystem.

⚡

Utility First

BISOVIA focuses on services designed around real everyday needs, with the platform growing through practical and useful experiences.

◎

Pi-Native

Pi is integrated into the platform's identity and payment experience, creating a foundation for Pi-enabled digital services.

🤝

Community

BISOVIA is designed to support participation, interaction, reputation and trust between users and service providers.

🧩

Expandable

The platform uses a modular architecture so new service areas can grow without changing the foundation of the Core.

Our approach

BISOVIA separates its stable Core from its service modules. The Core provides shared platform capabilities, while specialized modules handle individual service experiences.

The BISOVIA Core

The Core provides common capabilities used across the platform. This allows Mobility, Auto & Garages, Hospitality and Community to connect through consistent platform contracts.

Pi Identity & Auth Pi authentication and BISOVIA user identity.
Pi Wallet & Payments Payment creation, approval, completion and records.
User & Profile User profiles, roles, preferences and settings.
Trust & Safety Reputation, reviews, reports and moderation.
Notifications Important updates and service-related notifications.
API Gateway Standard contracts connecting the Core and service modules.

Growing through modules

BISOVIA is designed to expand through focused service modules while keeping the Core stable and reusable.
