ABOUT BISOVIA

A new platform for useful digital experiences.

BISOVIA is being developed as a modular digital platform focused on practical utility, community interaction and responsible integration with the Pi ecosystem.

OUR DIRECTION

Built with a clear foundation.

BISOVIA is designed to grow progressively instead of placing every feature into one complicated system.

01

Utility first

Features should solve real problems and provide meaningful value to users.

02

Simple experience

The interface should remain clear, accessible and easy to understand on both mobile and desktop.

03

Responsible Pi integration

Pi services will be integrated through official developer mechanisms and appropriate verification.

04

Future ready

The architecture is being prepared to evolve with new infrastructure and developer capabilities.

BUILDING BISOVIA

One stable layer at a time.

The project will move from frontend to backend, data services and Pi integration in controlled stages. Stability comes before complexity.
